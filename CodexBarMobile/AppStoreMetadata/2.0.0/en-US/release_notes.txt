CodexBar 2.0 is ready for iOS 27 and iPadOS 27, with an all-new data architecture that makes history, syncing, and navigation dramatically faster.

• Token Activity heatmaps reveal daily totals at a glance. Open the details to explore every provider, tap or hold any day, and browse up to a year of history.
• A new iPad experience uses wider, side-by-side layouts while keeping familiar bottom tabs.
• Sharing is completely redesigned with a preview-first editor, refreshed Classic and Vibe cards, and a new Heatmap card.
